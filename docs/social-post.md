### LinkedIn Post Copy

🛡️ Excited to share what we built for **KRITICAL HACKS ’26**: **ScamShield AI** — an evidence-grounded cyber investigation engine designed to protect students, recent grads, and freelancers from predatory job and internship scams.

According to consumer protection agencies, employment and advance-fee scams cost job seekers millions each year. Threat actors create shell entities, send convincing offer letters, and demand upfront "equipment assurance deposits" or banking details.

Traditional AI chatbots often hallucinate legitimacy when asked about obscure company names because they lack access to real-time web registries. We wanted to solve this by building a tool that doesn't just guess — it investigates.

🔍 **How ScamShield AI Works**:
1. **Live Web Research with TinyFish**: Ingests suspicious job offers or websites and invokes the **TinyFish Search and Fetch APIs** to scour live public records, corporate registries, and fraud databases in real time.
2. **Evidence Synthesis with Gemini 3.8 Flash**: Formulates structured risk profiles while maintaining strict separation between what the *user claimed* vs. what was *empirically observed online*.
3. **Four Calibrated Risk Tiers**: Categorizes findings into `HIGH CONCERN`, `NEEDS VERIFICATION`, `FEWER WARNING SIGNS FOUND`, and `INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE` — with zero false safety guarantees.
4. **Security & Privacy by Design**: All API keys remain strictly server-side, with built-in SSRF defenses, credential redaction, and prompt-injection safeguards.

💡 **Core Principle**: *"Don't just detect the warning. Investigate the evidence."*
